Data for the Solution of Ciphers in Spanish The Spanish language is written with the following alphabet: A B C CH D E F G H I J L LL M N Ñ O P Q R RR S T U V X Y Z while the exact sense often depends upon the use of accents over the vowels. However, in cipher work it is exceedingly inconvenient to use the permanent digraphs, CH, LL and RR and they do not appear as such in any specimens of Spanish or Mexican cipher examined. Accented vowels and Ñ are also not found and we may, in general, say that a cipher whose text is Spanish will be prepared with the following alphabet: A B C D E F G H I J L M N O P Q R S T U V X Y Z and the receiver must supply the accents and the tilde
